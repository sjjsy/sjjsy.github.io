This policy covers [sjj-aos](/sjj-aos/), a personal tool used only by its
owner, Samuel Sydänlammi.

## Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, sjj-aos accesses the owner's
Google Calendar events and calendars, Google Tasks lists and tasks, and the
owner's Google account email address.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own schedule and tasks and to
make changes the owner has approved. It is not used for advertising,
profiling or any other purpose.

## Storage and sharing

* The data is stored only on the owner's own computer. sjj-aos has no
  server, and its developer does not collect any data.
* The data is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties, except
  that the owner may have an AI assistant they use (such as Anthropic's
  Claude) process it to help plan their own time. Such processing follows
  that assistant provider's own terms.
* Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.

sjj-aos's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at
[Google Account → Third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)
and delete the local copy of the data.
