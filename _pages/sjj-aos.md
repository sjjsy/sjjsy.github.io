**sjj-aos** is Samuel Sydänlammi's personal, AI-assisted operating system for
planning time and tasks. It is a private tool used only by its owner and is
not offered to other users.

## What it does

* Reads its owner's Google Calendar events and Google Tasks lists into a local
  plain-text mirror on the owner's own computer, so that the owner and their
  AI assistant can see upcoming commitments next to the owner's to-do lists.
* Creates, moves or updates calendar events and tasks only when the owner
  has reviewed and approved each change.

It is built on the open-source [gog](https://github.com/openclaw/gogcli)
command-line client and a small local helper tool.
